## 👋 Who we are

Rukia Labs builds one platform for educational institutions and companies to **deliver, track, and prove learning** — for organisations training hundreds or thousands of people, from colleges running cohorts to companies certifying staff across sites.

Training doesn't scale the way software does: every new intake brings more marking, more follow-up and more reporting. We build the layer underneath the content, where that constraint actually sits.

Built first at **Zindua School**, a TVETA-registered college in Nairobi — our customer zero. Nothing ships to anyone else until it has survived a live intake there.

## 🧱 The platform

| Layer | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Core LMS** | Courses and content, cohort scheduling, assessments and rubrics, attendance and live sessions, certificates and learner records, invoicing and payment plans, compliance and audit reporting |
| **AI Intelligence** | AI-assisted grading with human review and override, drop-off risk flags, a 24/7 teaching assistant grounded in your courses, content generation and lesson scaffolding, delivery-quality signals |
| **Localisation** | M-Pesa collection with automatic reconciliation, eTIMS-compliant invoicing, offline-first delivery, WhatsApp reminders and microlearning, low-bandwidth video |

## 🎯 Who it's for

- **Educational institutions** — vocational training providers, private colleges and education initiatives (best fit: 100+ monthly active learners)
- **Companies** — L&D teams running employee, agent or partner training, with SSO, HRIS and Slack/Discord integrations, role-based pathways and competency-based tracking
